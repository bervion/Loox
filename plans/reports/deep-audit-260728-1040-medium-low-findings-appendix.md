### [MEDIUM][crypto-security] AutoFill domain matcher's substring clause offers real credentials on lookalike domains
- Vị trí: `VaultifyCore/VaultReader.swift:143`
- Evidence: `VaultItem.matchesAutoFill(serviceIdentifiers:)`:
```swift

    host == other || host.hasSuffix(".\(other)") || other.hasSuffix(".\(host)") || other.contains(host)
}
```
The final `other.contains(host)` clause is an unanchored substring test. With a stored website `google.com` and a service identifier `evilgoogle.com`, `"evilgoogle.com".contains("google.com")` is true; likewise `paypal.com.attacker.io` matches a stored `paypal.com`.
